Research Overview

My research focuses on understanding how individual cognition, social interactions, and evolving network structures shape collective behaviour in intelligent multi-agent systems.

I investigate the intersection of brain-inspired computation, social computing, trustworthy AI, and causal inference, with particular interest in learning, opinion formation, social influence, and the reliability of artificial societies.

My long-term research goal is to develop scientifically grounded and trustworthy artificial societies that can help investigate human-AI collective behaviour and support more reliable decision-making.
